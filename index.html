Download guide

Which links work?

Use a public link to the file itself. Quark share links open the official share page, where the owner’s permissions and Quark’s download options apply.

Can I download large files?

Linkjet has no file-size cap. The transfer runs directly between your browser and the file host. Available storage, host limits and your connection determine size and speed.

Why did my file open in a tab?

Some hosts display PDFs, images and videos in your browser. Use the browser’s download or Save As control to keep the file.
